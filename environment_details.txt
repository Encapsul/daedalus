Project Architecture:
- xbin-core: Library (~4000 lines) - format, compression, runtime detection, encryption, assembly
- xbin-cli: CLI (2331 lines in build.rs alone) - commands: build, run, sign, keygen, verify, inspect, doctor, scan, publish, upgrade, clean, completion
- stub: Launcher (2455 lines) - Linux-only ELF self-extracting launcher (only crate with unsafe)

---

.xbin Format:
- Layout: [stub][payload][metadata][footer]
- Magic: XBIN\x01, Footer magic: 0xBEEF_CAFE
- Integrity: SHA-256(payload || meta_bytes)
- Versions: v2 (plain), v3 (signed), v4 (encrypted), v5 (squashfs)

Python, Node.js, Deno, Java, Ruby, .NET/C#, Go, PHP (Laravel/Octane), Perl, Hugo, Binary (ELF/PE)

Critical Dependencies:

ed25519-dalek + zeroize (Ed25519 keys with zeroization)
aes-gcm + hkdf (AES-256-GCM encryption)
dirs (standard directories), human-panic (panic handler)
